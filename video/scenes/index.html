01 · Permissioned orchestration

Claude runs fleet through 32 MCP tools. You decide what each may do.

Every tool is allow, ask or forbid, per project. Git gates hold commit and push until you say yes.

Enforced by fleet, best-effort: not a sandbox.

    03 · Dispatch

    One , prompt. A sub-orchestrator takes it from there.

    Start a prompt with a comma: a hidden sub-orchestrator starts agents in their own worktrees, across your repos, and reports back. Your main Claude stays free.

    alt+o from any pane opens the head orchestrator in voice mode: hold space and talk. It switches projects and relays dispatches, local or over ssh.
